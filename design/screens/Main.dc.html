Build rules

  • Touch targets at least 44 × 44 pt, 8 pt between neighbours
  • Every swipe action has a visible button too
  • Reordering exercises offers Move up / Move down, not drag only
  • Motion 150–300 ms ease-out; honor Reduce Motion
  • Status uses colour plus an icon or word, never colour alone
  • Charts get a line style or direct label and a data-table fallback
